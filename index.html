02 About me

Hello

I make mobile games and apps. Since 2019 I take a product from the idea through to release: concept, mechanics, specification, schedule and quality.

For the last year I have worked solo, AI-first. Cursor and Claude Code cover development and design, so a game reaches release without a separate team.

03 Work experience

With a team and with AI

November 2019 — 2025 · MakeApp

With a team

I came up with concepts for small games and apps: puzzles, brain teasers, card and word games, sports, arcade, utilities, trackers and other genres. I wrote the specs, found contractors on Upwork, ran external and in-house developers, and kept schedule and quality. I communicated with contractors in English.

2025 — now · MakeApp

Me and AI

The same path, from idea to release, without developers or designers. I do the build, the store text and the graphics myself, with Cursor, Claude Code and ChatGPT.

Every game here is made from start to finish by me together with AI: idea, specs, code, graphics and store assets.

Cook Crusade

Match ingredients, defeat chefs!

A match-3 roguelike set in a cooking show. A young cook challenges nine master chefs: climb each chef's pantry row by row, open covered dishes (a battle, a basket of rewards or a burnt-pan trap) and grow stronger before the final duel.

Genre
Match-3 roguelike
Platform
Android Native

Soccer Ninja

Roll, dodge, score!

A 3D obstacle course in the spirit of American Ninja Warrior, starring a football. Roll the ball past windmills, sliding walls, pits, cannons and swinging hammers while a red laser wall chases you, then cross the finish line and watch it fly into the goal.

Genre
3D obstacle course
Platform
Android Native · Babylon.js

Killer Corner

Who was alone with the Baron?

A cozy murder-mystery logic puzzle. Read the suspects' statements, place everyone in the house so each row and column holds exactly one person, and the culprit is the only suspect left in the room with the sleepy Baron. 50 cases, notes, hints and a comic reveal of every motive.

Genre
Murder-mystery logic puzzle
Platform
Android Native · Jetpack Compose

Ball Barrage

Stop every wave!

A comedy tower defense on sports arenas. Grumpy beach balls, crazy ping-pong balls, a disco ball and a giant wrecking ball roll toward the goal, and you stop them with sports towers: football, tennis, basketball and many more. 50 levels, tower upgrades and a training center.

Genre
Sports tower defense
Platform
Android · Flutter · Three.js

Key Storm

Learn to type fast while you play

A game and a typing trainer in one: useful and fun at the same time. Several game modes, from falling words to races and laser battles, a daily challenge, five languages and juicy neon animations. Achievements, ranks and customization keep players coming back for one more round.

Genre
Typing arcade
Platform
Flutter

Breakway FC

Keep the ball. Beat the defense.

An endless pixel-art football runner full of speed and skill. Jump over cones and obstacles, slide under them, fake out defenders, pick the perfect moment to attack and finish with a goal — then do it again, faster and with style.

Genre
Endless football runner
Platform
Android Native · Phaser 3

World Tap

Find any country on the map

A geography quiz with an interactive world map. The game gives you a country name or a flag, and your task is to find it on the map. Countries, flags and mixed quizzes, every region of the world, lives and time modes.

Genre
Geography quiz
Platform
Android · Flutter
